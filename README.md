# «لا» الصعبة · The Hard No

A 15-minute browser simulation from Mosaad Al-Shalawi Academy on setting personal boundaries in everyday relationships. It opens on an editorial cover, then the learner chooses one of two characters:

- **نورة (Noura)**, their older sister, at home. She has left her children with them every Thursday "for two hours" for four months.
- **خالد (Khalid)**, their closest colleague, at work. Every Thursday before home time he asks them to finish "just this report".

Each reply changes the character's trust, shown in a four-state avatar. The end screen offers the other character for a second run.

## What it teaches

Four stages from the academy's boundaries guide:

1. **الوضوح (Clarity)**: a short, honest «لا» without over-justifying.
2. **صيغة «أنا» (I-statements)**: say what you need instead of judging her.
3. **الثبات (Holding)**: stay calm and consistent through guilt, tears, repetition and sulking.
4. **الالتزام (Follow-through)**: act on what you said, respect her feelings without carrying them, and offer only what you can sustain.

Traps: over-justifying or giving in, and accusing or threatening. Good moves: a clear boundary, an I-statement, and understanding without retreating. Two choices return the learner to an earlier stage.

## Notes for facilitators

- Noura, hidden layer 1: she enrolled in an evening diploma (Thursdays 6 to 10 pm) and told no one.
- Noura, hidden layer 2: she fears failing and hearing «قلنا لك» from the family, and she took the learner's own words a year ago («بيتي بيتك، وعيالك عيالي، أي وقت») as a standing agreement.
- Khalid, hidden layer 1: three months ago the manager gave him a second project with Finance on top of his own work, and he didn't dare refuse.
- Khalid, hidden layer 2: he's shortlisted for team supervisor and fears looking like he can't cope. He took the learner's words in his first week («لا تشيل هم، شغلك شغلي») as a standing agreement.
- The end screen shows the hidden story, two possible futures (common patterns, not predictions), a 12-point evaluation matrix, a decision review and two reflection questions.

## Running it

- Offline: open `index.html` in any modern browser. Everything (fonts, logo, avatars) is inside the file.
- GitHub Pages: Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > `main` / root. The bite is then at `https://<owner>.github.io/<repo>/`.
- `avatars/` holds the four SVG faces for each character, for use in slides.

## Privacy and assessment

Progress and reflections stay in the learner's own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere. The matrix is for reflection and discussion only. It has not been validated and does not measure ability or personality. The simulation is training, not therapy.
