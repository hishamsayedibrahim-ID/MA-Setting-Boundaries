# «لا» الصعبة · The Hard No

A 15-minute browser simulation from Mosaad Al-Shalawi Academy on setting personal boundaries in everyday relationships. The learner talks to Noura (نورة), their older sister, who has left her children with them every Thursday for four months. Each reply changes Noura's trust, shown in a four-state avatar.

## What it teaches

Four stages from the academy's boundaries guide:

1. **الوضوح (Clarity)**: a short, honest «لا» without over-justifying.
2. **صيغة «أنا» (I-statements)**: say what you need instead of judging her.
3. **الثبات (Holding)**: stay calm and consistent through guilt, tears, repetition and sulking.
4. **الالتزام (Follow-through)**: act on what you said, respect her feelings without carrying them, and offer only what you can sustain.

Traps: over-justifying or giving in, and accusing or threatening. Good moves: a clear boundary, an I-statement, and understanding without retreating. Two choices return the learner to an earlier stage.

## Facilitator notes

- Hidden layer 1: Noura enrolled in an evening diploma (Thursdays 6 to 10 pm) and told no one.
- Hidden layer 2: she fears failing and hearing «قلنا لك» from the family, and she took the learner's own words a year ago («بيتي بيتك، وعيالك عيالي، أي وقت») as a standing agreement.
- The footer's facilitator mode shows each option's type, the trust change, which layers have surfaced and a discussion prompt for every decision.
- The end screen shows the hidden story, two possible futures (common patterns, not predictions), a 12-point evaluation matrix, a decision review and two reflection questions.

## Running it

- Offline: open `index.html` in any modern browser. Everything (fonts, logo, avatars) is inside the file.
- GitHub Pages: Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > `main` / root. The bite is then at `https://<owner>.github.io/<repo>/`.
- `avatars/` holds the four SVG faces for use in slides.

## Privacy and assessment

Progress and reflections stay in the learner's own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere. The matrix is for reflection and discussion only. It has not been validated and does not measure ability or personality. The simulation is training, not therapy.
